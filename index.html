The Truth Comes Out

The Head Prefect's story didn't add up — because they made it up to cover for someone else. Under a little more pressure, they crack: the Founder's Day Cup was never stolen at all. The Math Club "borrowed" it for their annual Founder's Day puzzle hunt tradition, hiding it and scattering number-pattern clues across the school for anyone sharp enough to follow.

The Cup is exactly where it always was — behind a false panel in the trophy case, right where the first clue was hiding a locker key to prove it. Case closed, no harm done, and you cracked every clue along the way.
